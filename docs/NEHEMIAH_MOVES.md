# Nehemiah: hammer move map

## Heavy attacks

- **Down + Heavy — Foundation Breaker:** lift the mallet, smash the floor, then
  drive a second impact through the broken stones. Two hits share 29 base damage
  (35% first / 65% second); the first holds close, the second launches.
  Timing: 18 startup, 16 active, 30 recovery ticks.
- **Forward + Heavy — Rampart Rush:** brace the hammer and drive forward 50 units
  through large rubble. One hit per opponent, 31 base damage.
  Timing: 18 startup, 10 active, 30 recovery ticks. Solid walls stop movement.
- **Neutral + Heavy — Builder's Lift:** upward hammer swing.
- **Air Neutral/Forward + Heavy:** shared hammer recovery.
- **Air Down + Heavy:** descending pound; the stone burst occurs on landing.

Ground heavies charge while Heavy is held, up to 36 extra ticks. Release to
attack sooner. Holding a charge never grants invulnerability or early damage.
Left/right mirror both movement and hit lanes.

## Light attacks and links

Neutral Light is the close hammer opener. Forward Light sweeps ahead. Down Light
is a quick low foundation sweep; the stone-breaking combo belongs to Down Heavy.
Air Neutral, Forward and Down Light retain the hammer's aerial control.
A confirmed standing light can link to Forward/Down Light; confirmed aerial
lights can link into at most two aerial follow-ups. A miss cannot cancel freely.

## Visuals

Sixteen new registered hammer sprites cover the smash and rush sequences.
The floor smash produces two timed bursts. Heavy fragments spin, split near
their apex and fade to grit. Rubble is cosmetic, not an extra damaging projectile.
The Moves showcase presents the real attack timing and these control labels.
