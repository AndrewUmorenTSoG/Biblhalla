# Main game requirements

Source: production-pack/00_MASTER_PROMPT.md and canonical 09_Data.

Implementation order: fixed Q8 simulation and replay; 147 attacks and weapon lifecycle; campaign controllers/checkpoints; 12 fighters and six arenas; 84 levels and local multiplayer; menus, input, save recovery and accessibility; offline packaging and verification.

Runtime art will use distinct animated Canvas rigs and separately authored vector scenery as development art. Supplied move-sheet PNGs remain concept references, never collision or sprite atlases. Final hand-drawn animation production, balance review, signed iPad distribution and physical-device gates require separate completion evidence.
