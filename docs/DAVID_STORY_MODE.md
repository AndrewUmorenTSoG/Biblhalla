# Chapter One — David and Goliath

David is the playable fighter for the entire chapter. The flock encounters dramatize the lion and bear David recalls in 1 Samuel 17:34–37. The final fight draws on 1 Samuel 17:40–50. The upright claw fighters, their moves, and the multi-phase duel are game adaptations.

## Chapter route

| Part | Fight | Arena | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 001 · Defend the flock | 1. Lion Man | Wilderness Waystation | David starts with his sling. Learn to recognize a single raised paw, an open mouth, and a two-paw grab. A win unlocks the paired encounter. |
| 002 · Defend the flock | 2. Lion Man and Bear | Deborah’s Palm Court | Both opponents fight simultaneously, with two stocks apiece and independent attack sequences. The overhead crush creates the longest opening in this part. A win unlocks Elah. |
| 003 · The challenge at Elah | 3. Goliath | Elah Training Terrace | Goliath controls the ground with his spear, then adds a charge. Defeating him completes Chapter One. |

Each fight begins with a fresh three lives and a sling. Attacks deplete the opponent’s guard and increase their launch damage. Launch the opponent beyond the blast boundary to win. Guard thresholds change attack patterns; damage increases launch distance. Running out of lives or time allows a retry of the current fight. The chapter saves each victory. Continue starts at the next unlocked fight, and completed fights remain selectable for replay.

## Part 001 · Lion Man

A lion breaks cover at the edge of the flock. David steps into its path. The lion closes on two legs with his claws free. Stay near the middle while learning his reach. Near a ledge, the throw can cost David a life even while the lion has plenty of guard left.

| Move | Read the silhouette | What hits | David’s answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claw slash | One paw rises beside the mane for 30 ticks. | The forward claws cover 85 units for 10 ticks and deal 8 damage. | Step back as the paw rises. Return during the 25-tick recovery. |
| Fang lunge | The mouth opens wide for 34 ticks. | The lion advances 44 units during a 14-tick bite. His jaws reach another 120 units and deal 12 damage. | Jump the bite or cross behind him after he commits. He needs 34 ticks to recover. |
| Mane throw | Both paws reach together for 44 ticks. | Within 60 units, he catches David, holds him through the remaining active frames, then flings him forward for 15 damage and heavy knockback. A miss grabs nothing. | Back away or dodge the catch. A missed throw leaves 45 ticks to punish. |

**Fight rhythm.** At 75 guard, the lion opens with slash → bite → throw. Below 65% guard he opens with the bite. Below 30% he leads with the grab. Each phase resets the sequence, so read his paws after a big hit.

**Drawing key.** Read the [Lion Man sheet](../assets/runtime/story/lion.png) left to right. The first row shows two idle poses, a gathered running stride, and an extended stride. The second row completes the run and shows the slash windup and release. The third row shows open jaws, snapping jaws, the catch, and the release.

## Part 002 · Lion Man and Bear

The sheep scatter among the trees. Bear steps into the opening with short ears, broad shoulders, and a much heavier forearm than the lion’s. His opening swat tempts David into close range just before the overhead blow. Land one attack during a short recovery or use a long recovery to set up a sling shot.

| Move | Read the silhouette | What hits | David’s answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Side swat | One shoulder rolls back for 36 ticks. | One paw sweeps 105 units in front for 12 ticks and deals 11 damage. | Bait the swat, step outside it, and use the 32-tick recovery. |
| Overhead crush | One paw rises above his head for 54 ticks. | A tall strike covers 95 units forward and 155 vertically for 12 ticks. It deals 19 damage. | Cross behind him during the windup. The 52-tick recovery is the best heavy-attack opening. |
| Double rake | Both paws sink low for 40 ticks. | Both claws extend 145 units forward, only 65 high, for 18 ticks and deal 14 damage. | Jump clear, then counter during the 38-tick recovery. |

**Fight rhythm.** Bear starts at 105 guard and alternates swat → crush. Below 65% he inserts the rake between them. Below 30% the rake comes first, followed by swat → crush. The height of his paws tells David whether a jump is a good answer.

**Drawing key.** The [Bear sheet](../assets/runtime/story/bear.png) uses the same first six cells for idle and running. The next two show the side swat. The bottom row pairs the raised paw with the overhead impact, then the crouch with the extended rake. All attacks use bare claws.

## Part 003 · Goliath at Elah

David steps onto the terrace with his sling equipped. Goliath starts with 170 guard and a spear. At range, watch the point of the spear. At close range, keep an escape route above the floor.

| Phase | Pattern | How to fight it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening challenge | Spear thrust. A 40-tick tell precedes a 220-unit thrust for 14 damage. | Jump the point. Counter from behind or use the 35-tick recovery to release a stone. |
| Below 65% guard | Thrust → ground sweep. The sweep warns for 48 ticks and deals 15 damage across the floor. | Save a jump for the second attack. A shelf or a jump above the 42-unit sweep keeps David clear. |
| Below 30% guard | Champion charge → sweep → thrust. The charge locks David’s position, warns for 42 ticks, then travels for 24. | Move after the lane locks. Use the 54-tick charge recovery for a sling shot, then get above the following sweep. |

The game runs at 60 ticks per second. Easy, Hard, and Extremely Hard leave 60, 36, and 24 quiet ticks respectively between moves. Attack tells, hit damage, and recovery windows stay readable across difficulties.

Quick Battle also offers all three opponents for duels and boss encounters. Choose a roster fighter and an opponent separately. Exhibition results do not advance David’s chapter.
