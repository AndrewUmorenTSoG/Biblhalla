Damage makes the next hit launch farther. Falling beyond the arena costs one life.

01

Move & recover

Run, jump, then jump twice more in the air. An aerial heavy gives one recovery. Touch a solid wall to slide and jump away; wall assistance has a limit.

02

Pick your moment

Light attacks are faster. Grounded heavies are distinct signatures with real recovery. Hold Heavy to charge. Aim down for low attacks and aerial ground pounds.

03

Find your weapon

Start unarmed. Use Pickup near a bronze crate. Your weapons alternate with each new crate. Throw your weapon, or hold Down to drop it and recover it later.
