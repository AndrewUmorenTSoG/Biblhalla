# Campaign and optional challenge replay

The earlier optional anthology is replaced by the primary 84-level Story Archive campaign in `11_Story_Campaign`. Start with its master and index. Do not build a separate contradictory chronology or restrict the main story to six optional mini-games.

After completing a chapter, optionally replay its archive exercises with local medals, adjusted computer difficulty or a movement-only practice mode. Source context remains available independent of performance. All rewards are cosmetic/local, never combat stats, divine favor or proof of faith. Same-device co-op requires separately assigned controllers and a solo-capable companion fallback. No remote play is required or planned by this scope.

The standalone prototype has duel/waves/boss quick encounters only. It does not implement chapter progression, journal, companion co-op or these replay features. Keep implementation state visible in handoff reports.
