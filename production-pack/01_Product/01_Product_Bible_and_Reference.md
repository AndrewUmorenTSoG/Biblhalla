# Offline product brief and reference

Create an original biblical platform fighter with readable movement, directional attacks, damage/knockback, weapons, recovery and ring-outs. Brawlhalla is a genre/experience reference, not source code, artwork, exact physics or copied stage geometry. All supplied values and choreography are original design targets.

The product has two experiences: fictional sporting arena competition with the 12 supplied biblical fighters; and the 84-level Story Archive journey with fictional Dru/Ken companions, fixed biblical narration and labeled invented challenges. The story follows the main Genesis-to-resurrection arc. It includes group waves, single large bosses, rescues, traversal, puzzles and witness scenes. Jesus’ death/resurrection are fixed source-based scenes, not a combat challenge.

Every installed feature works offline: bots, all difficulties, levels, assets, audio/captions, save/replay and same-device local play. No account, subscription, remote leaderboard, cloud sync, ads or analytics. Quick battle has Easy / Hard / Extremely Hard; advanced match rules are behind More options. First play asks Keyboard / Touch / detected Controller. PC standalone and signed iPad package are distinct release artifacts.

The 12 fighters keep equal 15-point stat budgets, shared hurtboxes and authored kits. Arena moves are invented; historical identity does not create moral buffs. God, Jesus and the Holy Spirit are never playable or damageable. Miracles and sacred objects are not weapons or cooldown skills. No gore, religious mockery, superiority score or enemy faction defined by ethnicity/faith.

Delivered pack: prompts, 147 move records, six arena definitions, 12 fighter concept sheets, six map concepts and 84 level briefs. Separate prototype: actual solo computer combat with two fighters, three arenas and three encounter types; the full campaign/roster/local multiplayer remain production targets. A finished game requires runtime animation, full scene implementation, source review and measured physical-device tests.
