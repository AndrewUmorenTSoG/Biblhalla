# Generated artwork catalog

Artwork is original concept/reference material, not a finished sprite animation library or a physics diagram. Each character sheet has nine panels: identity, turnaround, one Light example, then six signature contact ideas. Written fighter files supply the panel keys and complete move timelines.

## Visual review and production limits

All 12 sheets were inspected for named identity, nine numbered panels, consistent general face/clothing, readable props and six distinct action intentions. Each of the six map paintings was inspected for the intended upper-platform count; Wilderness was corrected to one central platform. Illustrated poses can approximate the written contact intention, and decorative details vary. Exact limb trajectories, transparent production parts, full animation, atlas pivots, layered arena redraws, collision alignment and in-engine tests remain production work.

The rendered artwork uses opaque parchment backgrounds for sheets and full-color painted backgrounds for arenas. Do not crop nine cells and label the result an animation. Model proportions and props need artist approval at small in-game size. No physical-device gameplay performance or final game balance is claimed by generating these images.

Wilderness’s final delivered version includes a single upper platform and an open desert background. Its final edit instruction removed the extra platform and fortress, preserving the gold sandstone, teal cloth and flat side-view fighting ledge. Discarded intermediate art is not in this pack.
