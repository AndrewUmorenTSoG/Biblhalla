# Offline implementation phases and handoff

1. Read master/canonical data/offline/campaign prompts; produce requirement and asset-readiness matrices. Prove movement, legal inputs, one arena and fixed ticks.
2. Finish a real combat vertical slice with David/Samson, Easy/Hard/Extremely Hard computers, duel/waves/boss, stocks, damage, ring-outs, recovery, pickups, pause, result and rematch. Bundle assets into a player-launchable offline artifact. The supplied separate ZIP begins this phase with simplified mechanics and pose animation.
3. Build campaign chapter 1, one mixed-enemy wave scene, one three-phase boss, solo-capable companion task, source-aware witness scene and durable local save/retry. Review source/invention labels before repeating across chapters.
4. Expand all 12 arena fighters, eight weapon families/unarmed, six arenas and optional same-device local play. Implement complete animation coverage, remapping and accessible menus.
5. Build all 84 story levels in chapter order; track each as designed / implemented / playable / tested / reviewed. Keep the Gospel/finale scenes noncombat where specified. Integrate full regional map/actor/cutscene assets rather than pretending the six arena concepts are 84 campaign maps.
6. Package PC and signed iPad builds; test installed cold launch in airplane mode, touch/controller lifecycle, saves and memory/performance. Finish chapter replay, local export/import and release notes.

Handoff includes source, lock/build commands, standalone artifacts, content tables, manifests/prompts, implemented feature matrix, actual test output and unresolved limits. Design prompts alone are never a completed game. Physical device proof is separate from desktop emulation. No account service, online rooms or ranking is a later required phase; the product remains offline.
