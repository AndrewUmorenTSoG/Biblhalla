# Campaign art and map production brief

The supplied pack contains 12 fighter concept sheets and six arena concepts. The separate prototype adds two six-pose transparent atlases. The 84 campaign maps, protagonists, enemies and cutscenes below are generation/production briefs; they have not all been rendered or animated.

Create 14 regional kits: creation/garden; patriarchal roads and wells; family routes and Egyptian granaries; river/Egypt/sea/wilderness; Sinai/workshop/Jordan; judges’ countryside; Saul/David cliffs and Elah; Jerusalem/palace/divided roads; prophet/rivers/siege; Babylon/Persia/rebuilding; Nazareth/Bethlehem/Jordan; Galilee/hills/lake; Bethany/Jericho/Jerusalem; upper room/garden/cross viewpoint/tomb. Each level’s prompt identifies its unique view and interaction prop. Reuse architectural modules and palette within a chapter while changing routes, silhouettes and encounter rhythm.

For every kit request: 2560×1440 palette concept; layered sky/far/mid/near scenery; transparent platform surfaces with authored pivots; collision-free decorative props; NPC portrait expressions; objective/checkpoint shapes; restrained scene cards; and VFX with explicit lifespan. No image is a precise collider spec. Generate original maps with clear play planes; compile geometric collision from authored scene data, then align artwork in engine. Keep worship spaces and the finale free of arena combat.

Protagonist prompt: two original adult travelers Dru and Ken, distinct 4.5-head silhouettes, blue and green cloth accents, warm West Asian inspired costume materials, readable hands/feet, no halos or supernatural weapons. Produce consistent front/side/back turn-arounds, four expressions and isolated ordinary props. Follow with one animation task at a time; a character sheet alone is not a runtime animation atlas.

Chapter-card prompt: original hand-drawn storybook panorama of the chapter region, ink contours and warm cel shading, clear foreground path, subdued layered scenery, no logo copied from another game, no invented quotation, no sacred object as a weapon. See individual levels for local variations and event constraints.

Runtime budget: pack every required chapter asset with the installation; lazy decode from local bytes. No CDN fonts, remote image URLs, streaming narration or download-on-demand chapters. Asset manifest tracks source prompt, checksum, license, readiness, frame IDs and integration review. Test installed first launch with airplane mode enabled and caches cleared of developer artifacts.
