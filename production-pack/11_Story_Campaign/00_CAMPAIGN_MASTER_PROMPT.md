# Story campaign master — The Story Archive

Build a fully offline 84-level story adventure for Covenant Clash, from Genesis to the death and resurrection of Jesus. Read the main master, this campaign folder and `09_Data/campaign.json`. Deliver actual levels and save progression incrementally. The separate playable ZIP is an arena prototype; it does not implement this campaign.

Dru and Ken are fictional friends visiting a living illustrated Story Archive. Dru starts by thinking courage means winning every fight. Ken asks what strength is for. Across creation, promise, betrayal, deliverance, leadership, exile and Jesus’ ministry, their conversation develops toward protecting others, listening, mercy and hope. They do not time-travel to change Scripture, earn miracles, become prophets or supply secret missing events. Archive exercises are openly fictional; biblical accounts are presented as fixed source-based story scenes. The journey’s final movement is toward the cross, burial and resurrection announcement, with the weapons put away.

Structure: 14 chapters of six levels. Every chapter introduces a visual region and narrative question, advances through distinct mechanics, and ends in a meaningful resolution or transition. Combat chapters use group waves, one-boss arenas, protection objectives and positioning. Quiet sections provide emotional contrast, context, traversal, service and choices. Never add a filler arena fight to a scene merely to hit a combat quota. The campaign has 16 wave levels and 11 boss levels; count actual records if editing content.

Use level-specific prompts in `Levels/` as implementation briefs. Preserve the canonical account’s outcome. An invented Gate Warden can lose a training duel; defeating it must not rewrite the exile, Passover, crucifixion or resurrection. Goliath uses a special David reenactment with composure/positioning and one decisive stone, rather than a repeated-head-hit damage fight. Jesus, God and the Holy Spirit are never selectable combatants or health-bar enemies. Do not create spells from miracles or sacred objects.

Keep the core platform-fighter feel for action: movement, jump, recovery, dodge, light/heavy, weapon changes, accumulated damage and ring-outs. Boss guard, carry tasks, route interactions and escort bays are explicit campaign rules; they do not leak into local arena balance. Use the same input legality and hit ledgers. Same-device co-op is an optional production feature with two distinct assigned inputs; a companion AI must complete every required cooperation step in solo play. The supplied prototype is solo versus computers.

Present the main biblical narrative arc, not a claim to reenact every verse or every one of the 66 books. Wisdom and prophetic writings have labeled interludes; the Gospels provide the endpoint. Acts, the epistles and Revelation fall outside this campaign’s endpoint. Relative chronology is broad, and poetry, parables, retrospectives and books with uncertain dating are labeled. Do not invent exact dates to make the map look precise.

Build the campaign in releases: levels 1–6 with complete offline saves; one wave and one boss proof; chapters 2–6; chapters 7–10; Gospel chapters; finale and all completion/replay paths. A narrative review verifies reference accuracy, parable labels and distinction between narrator paraphrase and Bible quotes. Deliver reproducible builds, authored scene data, all local assets and actual playtest evidence. Never call unbuilt prompts completed levels.
