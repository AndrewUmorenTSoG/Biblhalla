# Original enemies and bosses

All routine opponents are fictional archive-training actors. They are not a claim that a biblical ethnic or religious group is inherently evil. Vary faces, skin tones and costumes; opposing silhouettes communicate weapon/role, not moral rank. Defeat uses sporting ring-out/retreat with no gore.

| ID | Silhouette / kit | Tell and counterplay | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| road_guard | Narrow tunic, short training sword | 18-tick light preparation, 26-tick lunge tell; bait and punish | Cannot chase across a gap without recovery planning |
| shield_guard | Broad rectangular shield, short staff | 30-tick shield push; upper route exposes the back | Guard does not cover 360 degrees; no permanent invulnerability |
| archer | Slim bow profile, cloth quiver | 30-tick raised-bow tell, marked direction; close the distance | Maximum two live arrows; no firing through solid walls |
| brute | Wide shoulder silhouette, ordinary mallet | 42-tick overhead windup, 36 recovery; dodge then counter | Slow approach, no difficulty damage multiplier |
| courier | Noncombat traveler with cloth bundle | Regroup and safe-bay marker | Never targetable, never used as bait |

Generate a separate turn-around, portrait and transparent pose sheet for each combat archetype. Pose names: idle, run, pivot, jump, fall, dodge, light startup/active/recovery, heavy startup/active/recovery, hit, ring-out retreat. Frame counts remain data from combat, not guessed from a contact sheet. Clean hands and feet, isolated weapon components, no baked-in trail as collision truth.

Bosses: Gate Warden, Grain Hoarder, Court Warden, Ridge Sentinel, Lion drill, Gate Captain, Bear drill, Goliath, Pursuer Captain, Siege Captain and Court Warden variant. Each individual level supplies three distinct phases. Reuse a role’s base silhouette only with new attack cadence, arena interaction and clear costume variant. Goliath receives a unique imposing historical concept and a restrained one-stone reenactment. Animal drills avoid gore. Sacred figures never appear in this enemy catalog.

Write frame data for new boss attacks with startup/active/recovery, fixed-direction lock tick, hazard lifetime, reachable safe zones and punish windows. Contact tests verify the drawn weapon envelope, one-hit-per-target, dodge legality, no contact after a phase exit and no lingering hazard on Retry. All generated art remains concept status until frames, pivots and visual/collision review are complete.
